Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 31, 2026

Second Line AI respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Second Line AI collects, uses, stores, and discloses information when you visit our website, contact us, book a meeting, use a demonstration, or receive services from us.

By using our website or services, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We May Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Name
  • Business name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Service address
  • Company website
  • Appointment information
  • Information submitted through forms
  • Information shared during calls, meetings, or demonstrations
  • Customer-service information
  • Billing and transaction information
  • Information necessary to configure or provide our services

We may also collect technical information, including:

  • Internet Protocol address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Referring website
  • Date and time of access
  • Website interaction data
  • Cookies and similar technologies

2. Call and Communication Information

Calls involving AI-powered systems may be processed, transcribed, summarized, or recorded depending on the configuration and applicable law.

Information collected during calls may include:

  • Caller name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Service address
  • Reason for calling
  • Appointment preferences
  • Call transcript
  • Call recording
  • Call summary
  • Other information voluntarily provided by the caller

Clients using Second Line AI services are responsible for determining and implementing any disclosures, notices, or consents required for their business, jurisdiction, industry, and use of call recording or automated communication.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Provide and improve our services
  • Respond to inquiries
  • Schedule appointments
  • Configure AI-powered systems
  • Deliver call summaries
  • Process payments
  • Communicate with clients and prospects
  • Maintain security
  • Prevent fraud or misuse
  • Analyze website performance
  • Improve customer experience
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Enforce agreements
  • Develop new services and features

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We may use third-party service providers for website hosting, email, scheduling, telephony, artificial intelligence processing, workflow automation, payment processing, analytics, customer relationship management, data storage, and technical support.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and contractual obligations.

Third-party platforms may include providers such as Google, OpenAI, Vapi, Twilio, Cal.com, n8n, payment processors, hosting providers, and other software or infrastructure vendors.

5. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to operate the website, remember preferences, understand usage, and improve performance.

You may control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

6. How We Share Information

We may share information:

  • With service providers supporting our operations
  • With contractors or professional advisers

We do not sell personal information for money.

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal, accounting, tax, and security obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it was collected.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect information.

However, no website, communications system, artificial intelligence platform, telephony provider, storage system, or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Your Choices

You may:

  • Request access to certain personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion where legally applicable
  • Opt out of marketing emails
  • Decline nonessential communications
  • Adjust browser cookie settings

Service-related communications may still be necessary while you are a client.

If you provide a mobile number and consent to text communications, Second Line AI may send messages relating to your inquiry, appointment, service, or account.

Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply.

You may reply STOP to opt out of promotional text messages and HELP for assistance where supported.

Our website and services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
