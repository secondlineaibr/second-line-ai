A Revenue Recovery System Built Around Your HVAC Company

Second Line AI does not hand you a generic chatbot and expect your team to figure it out. We map your call flow, configure Marie around your company, test the experience, and refine the system based on real operational needs.

Book Your Free Audit
1

Revenue Recovery Audit

We begin with a focused review of your current inbound-call process. We examine business hours, call volume, missed-call procedures, after-hours coverage, dispatch process, scheduling rules, customer intake, emergency escalation, follow-up speed, existing software, and current staffing constraints.

The purpose is to identify where opportunities may be delayed or lost.

2

Call-Flow Design

We document how Marie should respond to the types of calls your company receives — new service requests, no-cooling calls, existing-customer concerns, maintenance requests, replacement estimates, financing questions, warranty questions, complaints, vendor calls, employment inquiries, calls outside your service area, and calls requiring human escalation.

Marie does not improvise your policies. She follows the business rules you approve.

3

Custom Configuration

We configure the system using your company information: greeting, tone and communication style, services offered and not offered, service territory, office hours, diagnostic fees, appointment availability, arrival expectations, emergency procedures, escalation contacts, FAQs, customer notifications, call summaries, and scheduling or CRM integrations.

4

Testing and Quality Control

Before launch, we test Marie against multiple call scenarios: straightforward service booking, a caller with incomplete information, an upset customer, a caller outside the service area, an after-hours emergency request, a replacement estimate inquiry, a financing question, an existing-customer follow-up, a caller requesting a technician's advice, and a caller trying to bypass company policy.

We refine the system until the conversation follows your approved process.

5

Launch and Optimization

Once approved, Marie can begin supporting your selected call flow — after-hours calls, weekend calls, overflow calls, missed-call recovery, specific call types, or broader inbound-call coverage. We then review performance and refine the system as needed.

What Marie Does

  • Answers calls according to your approved instructions.
  • Collects customer information.
  • Identifies the reason for the call.
  • Communicates approved company information.
  • Helps route the caller toward the proper next step.
  • Documents call outcomes.
  • Supports your existing team.

What Marie Does Not Do

  • Diagnose HVAC equipment.
  • Claim to be a licensed technician.
  • Guarantee appointment availability unless connected to approved scheduling data.
  • Promise pricing not authorized by your company.
  • Provide emergency, legal, medical, or safety advice.
  • Replace your responsibility to review and follow up with customers.
  • Guarantee revenue or business results.
