Meet Marie

Marie is an AI-powered HVAC receptionist designed to sound warm, calm, capable, and professional—without pretending to be a technician.

Call Marie: 225-325-2700

The First Voice Your Customer Hears Matters

When a homeowner calls an HVAC company, they are often uncomfortable, frustrated, worried, or in a hurry. Marie is designed to begin with empathy, gather the right information, and keep the conversation moving.

Her role is to help the caller feel heard while collecting the details your team needs.

How Marie Communicates

  • Uses short, natural sentences.
  • Speaks at a comfortable pace.
  • Sounds professional without sounding scripted.
  • Acknowledges the caller's problem.
  • Uses the caller's name once it is known.
  • Explains only information approved by the contractor.
  • Avoids exaggerated Southern expressions.
  • Avoids pretending to have technical expertise.
  • Clearly confirms the next step.
EXAMPLE

“I'm sorry y'all are dealing with that. Let me get a few details so we can figure out the best next step.”

What Marie Can Collect

Depending on the contractor's workflow, Marie may collect:

  • Customer name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Service address
  • City, state, ZIP code
  • New or existing customer status
  • Type of service request
  • Description of the issue
  • Preferred appointment window
  • Access instructions
  • Whether someone will be home
  • Relevant safety or escalation information
