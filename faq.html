Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about Marie, the Revenue Recovery Desk, and how Second Line AI works.

What is Second Line AI?

Second Line AI is an AI automation company that builds call-handling and revenue recovery systems for home-service businesses, beginning with Louisiana HVAC contractors.

What is a Revenue Recovery Desk?

A Revenue Recovery Desk is an AI-powered system designed to help answer calls, capture customer information, support scheduling workflows, document service opportunities, and reduce the number of qualified calls lost to voicemail or delayed follow-up.

Who is Marie?

Marie is the name of Second Line AI's AI receptionist. She can be customized around an HVAC company's business name, services, territory, fees, hours, call-handling procedures, and communication style.

Can I call Marie before booking a meeting?

Yes. Call the live demo at 225-325-2700. The demo uses a fictional HVAC company and is intended to show how a customer conversation may sound.

Does Marie tell customers that she is a technician?

No. Marie does not claim to be a technician and should not provide technical diagnoses. She is designed to gather information, communicate approved company policies, and guide callers toward the correct next step.

Can Marie schedule appointments?

Marie may be able to offer or request appointment windows depending on the contractor's scheduling system, integration, availability, and approved workflow. The exact scheduling process is customized for each business.

Can Marie answer calls after hours?

Yes. Marie can be configured for after-hours, weekend, overflow, missed-call, or broader call coverage.

Can Marie handle emergency calls?

Marie can gather information and follow an approved escalation process. She does not replace emergency services, technicians, licensed professionals, or the contractor's responsibility to establish appropriate safety procedures.

Will Marie replace my office staff?

Second Line AI is not designed around replacing valuable employees. Marie can support office staff by handling repetitive intake, overflow calls, after-hours requests, and information capture.

Can Marie work with ServiceTitan or Housecall Pro?

Integration options depend on the client's software, account permissions, available APIs, workflow requirements, and technical feasibility. Potential integrations are reviewed during the audit and implementation process.

How long does implementation take?

Implementation time depends on the complexity of the call flow, number of integrations, availability of client information, testing requirements, and requested features. A timeline is provided before implementation begins.

Does Second Line AI guarantee more revenue?

No. Second Line AI does not guarantee revenue, sales, profits, appointment volume, or specific business outcomes. Results depend on multiple factors, including call volume, customer demand, staff follow-up, pricing, service quality, market conditions, and the contractor's sales process.

What is included in the free audit?

The 17-minute Revenue Recovery Audit includes a focused review of your current call process, possible revenue leakage points, applicable automation opportunities, and a live demonstration.

How much does Second Line AI cost?

Pricing depends on the scope of the system, call volume, integrations, usage, implementation requirements, and ongoing management needs. Pricing is discussed after the Revenue Recovery Audit.

Do I need to replace my current phone number?

Not necessarily. Depending on the setup, calls may be forwarded, routed, or handled through an approved telephony configuration. The recommended approach depends on your current phone system.

Is the demo number a real HVAC company?

No. The demo uses a fictional HVAC company for demonstration purposes.

How do I get started?

Book a free 17-minute Revenue Recovery Audit at cal.com/secondline.ai/17-minute-revenue-audit. You may also email bookmore@secondlineai.com.
