More than an AI receptionist

Your HVAC Revenue Recovery Desk

Marie is not designed to replace your company, your technicians, or the relationships you have built with your customers. She is designed to protect the opportunities your business is already generating.

Marie can be configured to:

  • Answer inbound calls when your office is busy or closed.
  • Greet callers using your company name and preferred tone.
  • Acknowledge the customer's problem before beginning intake.
  • Collect names, phone numbers, service addresses, and issue details.
  • Identify whether the caller is a new or existing customer.
  • Follow your approved service-area and scheduling rules.
  • Offer approved appointment windows when connected to your workflow.
  • Explain approved diagnostic fees and company policies.
  • Escalate urgent situations according to your instructions.
  • Send organized call summaries to your team.

Second Line AI builds the system around your company—not the other way around.
