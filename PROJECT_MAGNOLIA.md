# 1. Executive Vision

Second Line AI exists to become the most trusted Revenue Recovery company serving home service businesses.

We begin by serving Louisiana HVAC contractors.

Every interaction should communicate professionalism, trust, hospitality, craftsmanship, and continuous improvement.

The website must not feel like a typical software company, AI agency, automation agency, chatbot company, or virtual receptionist service.

It should feel like the digital headquarters of a premium Louisiana Revenue Operations company.

Visitors should believe they are meeting professionals who genuinely understand the operational challenges of running an HVAC business.

Technology is never the hero.

The Louisiana HVAC contractor is the hero.

Artificial intelligence simply enables a better customer experience and more consistent business operations.

The primary purpose of the website is to earn enough trust that a qualified Louisiana HVAC owner books a Complimentary Revenue Recovery Assessment™.

Every page, section, image, interaction, animation, and call to action must support that objective.

We are not building the best AI website in the HVAC industry.

We are building the most trusted digital experience an HVAC owner has ever had.

Every design decision, every interaction, every word, every image, every animation, and every automation must support that objective.

# 2. Company Positioning

Second Line AI is a premium Revenue Operations company specializing in Revenue Recovery for Louisiana HVAC contractors.

We help HVAC businesses recover more of the revenue they are already paying to generate by improving how opportunities are captured, qualified, scheduled, followed up with, and measured.

Artificial intelligence is one of the technologies we use.

It is not our identity.

Revenue Recovery is our identity.

The company must never be positioned as:

- An AI agency
- An automation agency
- A chatbot company
- A virtual receptionist company
- A generic software vendor

Second Line AI owns the category:

Revenue Recovery.
